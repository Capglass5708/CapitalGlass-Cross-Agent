# CG Master Graph Constitution v1

Constitution ID: `CG-MASTER-GRAPH-CONSTITUTION-v1`

## Purpose

Define the durable principles that govern the CG Master Graph and all systems that contribute to or consume it.

## Constitutional principles

1. The graph connects authoritative systems; it does not replace them.
2. Every accepted fact and relationship must retain provenance.
3. Facts, observations, claims, inferences, and decisions are distinct concepts.
4. Historical lineage is preserved through versioning and supersession, not destructive overwrite.
5. Every canonical entity has a stable ID and an accountable owner or authority domain.
6. Every contribution identifies its producer, producer version, source, source authority, and exact evidence anchor when available.
7. Uncertainty must be represented explicitly.
8. Conflicts must be held, merged, rejected, or superseded by deterministic policy.
9. Repositories and extractors may propose knowledge but may not independently promote canonical graph truth.
10. Generated releases must be deterministic from identical canonical inputs.
11. Sensitive information, secrets, and unrestricted raw content are excluded from graph payloads.
12. The original artifact remains in its authoritative store; the graph retains identity, lineage, metadata, and relationships.
13. CG-EDF discovers and documents objective state; architectural decisions are recorded separately.
14. Data-Extraction is a primary observation producer, not the final authority for identity or truth.
15. Cross-Agent governs harvest, discovery, review, and publication coordination without replacing repository ownership.
16. Every material graph transition must produce evidence and a closeout verdict.
17. The graph must support bounded retrieval and avoid broad raw scans when authoritative indexes are available.
18. Retired entities remain discoverable as historical nodes.
19. Human review is required where automated identity, authority, or sensitivity resolution is insufficient.
20. Growth must improve explainability, not merely increase node count.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

The graph must not become:

- A password, secret, or credential store
- A raw chat or binary archive
- A replacement for project, proposal, document, image, or application databases
- An unrestricted crawler of personal or system content
- An embedding dump
- A source of silent autonomous truth promotion
- A visualization-only project without authoritative contracts

## Amendment rule

Changes require a versioned decision record that explains the reason, consequences, compatibility impact, and any superseded principle.
